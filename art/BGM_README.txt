Background music (optional)

Place either:
  ../bgm.mp3   (same folder as index.html), or
  bgm.mp3 / bgm.ogg in this art/ folder

The HTML loads ./bgm.mp3 first, then ./art/bgm.mp3.

Use a track you have rights to — e.g. search “cute casual vocal” or “happy acoustic vocal” on royalty-free sites (Pixabay, Uppbeat, YouTube Audio Library) and download as MP3.

The game loops it after you press Play and pauses it when the game is paused or over.
